About Alcove

Discover the story
of digital art

A search engine for blockchain creativity.

The Problem

Digital art on blockchains is one of the most exciting creative movements of our time — but discovering it is terrible. Marketplace search is keyword-only, fragmented across chains, and drowning in speculation noise.

Artists and collectors deserve better.

What Alcove Does

Alcove searches across Ethereum and Tezos simultaneously, combining results into a single beautiful grid. Describe a feeling or concept and our AI interpreter translates your vision into optimized searches.

Our Sources
OBJKT (Tezos)
The richest art ecosystem in crypto. Generative art, photography, illustration, glitch, abstract, and experimental work. Search by title, description, tags, collection, or artist name.
Alcove is an independent project. All art is displayed with links back to their original marketplaces and creators.
